When Robert asks for a single bookkeeping step — "move A.001.6 to initiatives" — do exactly that step. Don't also draft the write-up, relink the row, or start the next stage, even when `session-modes.md` says promotion "also" needs them. Name the follow-on steps in the reply and let him choose.

**Why:** On 2026-10-17 I bundled a write-up and a row link into a promotion; he interrupted: "All I want you to do is move it to initiatives, don't start now." He then asked for the write-up separately once he'd decided.

**How to apply:** Process docs list what a stage *eventually* needs, not what to do in this turn. Pairs with CLAUDE.md "Don't jump ahead" and [[feedback-session-modes]].
